Days 2–3
Sat–Sun, June 13–14 · Family time near Bishop
Family

Two open days in the Owens Valley with family — no fixed plans. The high Sierra is right there whenever you want it: Bishop Creek lakes, the Bristlecone Pines, or just an easy valley day.

Both nights covered by the Palisades Lodge booking above (revised checkout Sun Jun 14).
🍴 Eat — Bishop (the best food town of the trip)
  • Pupfish Cafe — healthiest pick: build-your-own plates, avocado toast, salmon, salads. Breakfast/lunch, closed Wed.
  • The Rolling Chef 395 — 4.9★ food truck, fresh fish/shrimp tacos, fresh cucumber juice. Weekdays only.
  • Cloudripper — local-sourced pop-up at Owens Valley Distillery (Thu–Sat eve) — bibimbap, noodles. Check their IG first.
  • Mountain Rambler Brewery — reliable daily sit-down: burgers, salads, Brussels sprouts.
  • Erick Schat's Bakkery — famous stop; grab sheepherder bread & sandwiches here for the drive days ahead.
🛒 Stock up before you leave Bishop: the next two days cross long empty stretches (Goldfield, Tonopah, Cathedral Gorge) with few options. Load a cooler with sandwiches, fruit, water — Schat's bread + Pupfish make a great picnic kit.

Travel

Day 5
Tuesday, June 16 · Zion National Park (day trip)
Park Day

The crown jewel. An easy day trip from the farm into Zion — towering sandstone, the Virgin River, scenery that stops you mid-sentence. Beat the heat with an early start and water-based hikes, then back to the farm for the night.

  • EarlyDrive Cedar City farm → Zion (Springdale) (~50 min; verify exact on map); no checkout — you're at the farm again tonight
  • MiddayZion shuttle up-canyon — Riverside Walk into the Narrows (wading the river = perfect hot-day kid activity)
  • Aft.Emerald Pools or the easy Pa'rus Trail; canyon junction views
  • TipRuns hot in June — lots of water, early start, midday in shade or the river
  • Eve.Drive back to the Mini Animal Farm (~50 min)
🛏 Second night at the Mini Animal Farm (covered by the Nights 4–5 booking above). The kids get the animals morning and evening, and you skip packing up.
🍴 Eat — Springdale / Zion
  • Before the park: FeelLove Coffee opens 6a — coffee, vegan/GF baked goods, and healthy grab-and-go. Or The Park House (4.8★ breakfast, fresh fruit & omelets) if you want a sit-down start.
  • Dinner: Oscar's Cafe — the Zion institution (daily 7a–9p, vegan items, big salads, sweet potato fries, patio) or Whiptail Grill for fresh Mexican.
🛒 Bring lunch into Zion: there's only one café (Zion Lodge) inside the park and it's busy. Grab sandwiches + lots of water at FeelLove or Sol Foods (995 Zion Park Blvd, daily 7a–11p) before you board the shuttle — June is hot and the Narrows/Riverside walk has no food. Pack more water than you think.
